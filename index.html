About Me

I'm here to be
beside you.

With over twelve years of clinical experience, I believe every individual carries their own unique story. I provide a safe, non-judgemental space where you can explore your inner world and rediscover your strengths.

I specialise in anxiety disorders, depression, trauma, and relationship difficulties, drawing on evidence-based approaches tailored specifically to each person I work with.

  • 🌿
    Integrative Approach I address cognitive, emotional, and somatic dimensions together.
  • 🔒
    Confidentiality & Trust All sessions are conducted with the strictest confidentiality.
  • 🌱
    Personalised Care Every therapy plan is tailored to your individual needs and goals.

The Process

How does it work?

A clear, transparent process so you always know what to expect. No surprises — just steady, supportive progress.

01

Free Consultation

A complimentary 20-minute introductory call to discuss your needs, answer your questions, and see if we're the right fit.

02

Assessment

In our early sessions I carry out a thorough assessment and co-create a personalised therapy plan with you.

03

Therapy

Weekly sessions working towards your agreed goals, using evidence-based techniques suited to your needs.

04

Progress & Closure

We regularly review your progress together and shape the journey as you grow — finishing when you feel ready.
